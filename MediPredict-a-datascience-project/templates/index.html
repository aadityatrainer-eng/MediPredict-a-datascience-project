The Challenge: Late Disease Detection

Healthcare faces critical challenges that impact patient outcomes and treatment costs

⚠️

Late Detection

Delayed disease diagnosis significantly increases health risks for patients, often leading to more severe complications and reduced treatment options.

⏱️

Manual Analysis

Traditional manual analysis is time-consuming for doctors dealing with large datasets, creating bottlenecks in patient care and diagnosis.

💰

Higher Costs

Delayed diagnosis leads to significantly increased treatment expenses, placing financial burden on both patients and healthcare systems.

Data Collection & Sample Dataset

Structured medical data gathering for disease prediction

📊

Data Sources

Hospital records and public healthcare datasets providing comprehensive patient information for analysis and prediction.

🔍

Data Types

Demographic data (Age), Clinical measurements (BMI, Blood Pressure), and Lab Results (Glucose, Cholesterol) for comprehensive assessment.

🧹

Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

Handle missing values, remove duplicates, correct outliers, and convert categories to ensure accurate and suitable data for analysis.

Key Health Parameters

Important health parameters used for disease prediction

🎂

Age

Patient age as a fundamental risk factor for disease prediction and assessment.

⚖️

Body Mass Index (BMI)

Weight-to-height ratio indicating obesity and metabolic health status.

💓

Blood Pressure

Systolic and diastolic measurements for cardiovascular health assessment.

🍬

Glucose Level

Blood sugar measurements critical for diabetes risk prediction.

🧪

Cholesterol

Total cholesterol levels indicating cardiovascular disease risk.

💗

Heart Rate

Resting heart rate as an indicator of cardiovascular fitness and health.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Revealing patterns and relationships in healthcare data

📊

Visualizations

Bar charts, line graphs, histograms, and scatter plots to understand data distributions and trends in patient health metrics.

🔗

Correlation Analysis

Measure feature dependency to identify relationships between health parameters and their impact on disease risk.

💡

Key Insights

Higher glucose levels linked to diabetes risk, age correlation with disease prevalence, and BMI impact on overall health outcomes.

🎯

Feature Selection

EDA helps select the most relevant features, significantly improving machine learning model performance and prediction accuracy.

Model Development & Evaluation

Building and validating machine learning models for healthcare prediction

🎯

Feature Selection

Identified important health parameters: Age, BMI, Blood Pressure, Glucose Level, Cholesterol, and Heart Rate for optimal disease prediction.

🤖

Model Building

Dataset split into training and testing sets. Applied algorithms including Logistic Regression and Decision Trees for accurate risk predictions.

✅

Model Evaluation

Performance validated using Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and Confusion Matrix metrics to ensure reliability before deployment.
